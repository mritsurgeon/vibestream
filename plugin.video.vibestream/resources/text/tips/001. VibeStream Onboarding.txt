Welcome to VibeStream!

This addon is designed for high-performance streaming with a focus on simplicity and quality.

[B]Key Features:[/B]
1. [B]Auto-Fallback Playback[/B]: If a stream buffers, VibeStream will automatically find a smaller/more stable source.
2. [B]Quality Presets[/B]: Choose your internet profile in Tools -> Setup Wizard to filter sources automatically.
3. [B]Trakt Personal Keys[/B]: Using your own API keys prevents Trakt "Rate Limit" errors during peak hours.

[B]Troubleshooting:[/B]
- If you experience buffering, try lowering your Quality Preset in Settings.
- If scraping is slow, ensure you have Real-Debrid authorized.
- Check the official Kodi Wiki for general app issues.

Happy Streaming!
